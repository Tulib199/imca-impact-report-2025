Our story

A Pillar of Faith & Service

03

For more than five decades, IMCA has built institutions that nurture faith, educate the next generation, and serve neighbors of every background.

From Masjid Al-Fajr—Indiana’s first Sunni mosque—and MTI School of Knowledge, the state’s first Islamic K–12 school, IMCA leads spiritual and educational life in Central Indiana.

Beyond worship and classrooms: food assistance, emergency aid, Janazah care, free clinics, prison ministry, new-Muslim support, and civic partnership.

Mission

Building a vibrant, healthy, sustainable Muslim community in Central Indiana—anchored in faith, celebrating our diverse heritage, and placing youth at the forefront.

Vision

Serving Central Indiana since 1970—united in worship, learning, and care for the Ummah and the wider city.

Marriage, Families & Janazah

07

From Nikah to bereavement—helping families stay healthy and together, and ensuring no one grieves alone.

Marriage · 20+

Premarital counseling, Nikah, post-marriage counseling, and legal documentation.

Goal: reduce divorce cases and family crises.

Family Support · 170+

Family counseling, crisis intervention, legal assistance, and domestic violence prevention & intervention.

Goal: keep families healthy and together.

Youth Support · 100+

Religious counseling, suicide prevention, mental health, drug-addiction assistance, parent–youth relationships, weekly halaqahs, monthly programs.

Mission: invest in youth to build our future.

Janazah · 100+

Spiritual care for dying Muslims and families—Janazah services, bereavement counseling, and grief support.

  • 100+ Janazah services conducted annually
  • Complex cases: 5 hospice · 2 homicides · 1 suicide
  • $44,000 Janazah aid (2020–2025), up to $4,600 per funeral

Objective

No one should grieve alone.

Building a Healthy Community

10

Collaborative health initiatives address physical, mental, and spiritual well-being for members and neighbors.

Crescent Medical Clinic

In partnership with the Hoosier Crescent Foundation—free medical services for uninsured individuals of all faiths at Masjid Al-Fajr (typically third Saturday, 10 AM–2 PM).

  • Volunteer physician consultations
  • Medical student training & support
  • Discounted lab testing via Lab-On-Demand
  • Prescription guidance · social needs screening

Clinic founding year and lifetime patient count to be confirmed · hcfcorp.org
